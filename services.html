Services

Detailing services built around the finish

Protection, restoration and careful cleaning, inside and out. Each service is described below, and every vehicle would be assessed before any work begins.

01 · Protection

Ceramic Coating

A liquid-applied protective layer that bonds to the paint once it has been properly prepared. It is designed to add depth and gloss and to make routine washing easier.

Key benefits

  • Enhanced gloss and depth of colour
  • Easier, faster maintenance washes
  • Helps resist road film, water spots and everyday contamination
  • Applied over carefully prepared paint

Best suited to new vehicles, or paint that has just been corrected. Results depend on preparation, product choice and aftercare.

02 · Restoration

Paint Correction

Careful machine polishing of the clear coat to reduce swirl marks, light scratches and oxidation. The depth of correction depends on the condition and thickness of the paint, so it is assessed first.

Key benefits

  • Reduced swirl marks and light surface defects
  • Improved clarity and reflection
  • An excellent base before a coating or sealant
  • An approach guided by the paint's condition

Best suited to paint that looks dull or hazy under sunlight. Deep scratches that pass through the clear coat cannot be fully polished out.

Request a Quote for paint correction See packages
03 · Cabin care

Interior Detailing

Thorough cleaning and conditioning of every surface inside the cabin: seats, carpets, dashboard, door cards and glass, with care taken around controls and sensitive materials.

Key benefits

  • Deep cleaning of fabric, carpet and leather
  • Conditioning for leather and trim
  • Streak-free interior glass
  • A fresher, cleaner cabin to drive in

Best suited to daily drivers, family vehicles, or a vehicle being prepared for sale.

Request a Quote for interior detailing See packages
04 · Wash and protect

Exterior Detailing

A careful multi-step wash with decontamination to lift bonded dirt, followed by a sealant or wax that enhances gloss and adds a layer of protection.

Key benefits

  • A wash method designed to reduce the risk of new marks
  • Decontamination for a smooth, clean surface
  • Wheels, tyres, trim and glass cleaned
  • Gloss-enhancing protection to finish

Best suited to regular upkeep, or as preparation before polishing or coating.

How it works

A typical visit, step by step

A straightforward process from the first conversation to the final walkthrough.

  1. Consultation

    Discuss the vehicle, what you would like improved and which services suit it.

  2. Inspection and prep

    Assess the paint and interior, then wash and decontaminate before any treatment.

  3. Treatment

    Polishing, coating or deep cleaning, depending on the services chosen.

  4. Final inspection

    A last check under good light and a walkthrough of the finished work.
