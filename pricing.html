What shapes a final price

Vehicle size and type

A compact coupe and a large SUV need different amounts of time and product.

Paint and interior condition

Heavier swirl marks or a well-used interior may call for extra work.

Extras and scheduling

Optional extras would be quoted separately, with timing agreed in advance.
